title: "Privacy"
description: "How Vadimus counts its readers, and how to opt out."
---
Vadimus counts its readers to see which pages are read and how people find them. It sets no cookies and never stores your IP address. [How the counting works, and your rights](https://3ft9.com/privacy/analytics).

<p><label><input type="checkbox" id="v-opt-out"> Don't count my visits to this site</label></p>

Ticking the box stores the date you ticked it in this site's local storage, and nothing else. While it is there, your browser sends us nothing. Nothing is sent either when your browser sends the Global Privacy Control signal.

If you choose light or dark mode in the footer, your browser remembers that choice for this site in the same way. It goes back to following your system setting when you choose the mode your system already uses.
